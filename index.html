About Superpack

Superpack is a next‑generation platform designed for capturing real‑world moments and sharing them instantly. Built with simplicity and creativity in mind, Superpack focuses on real‑life video recording and seamless sharing.

While future versions may support uploading videos from files, this early concept focuses on the core experience: recording life as it happens.

Jacob Hurst

CEO of Superpack

Inspired by Ducky Web

Superpack draws inspiration from the creativity and simplicity of Ducky Web — another GitHub‑powered project that encourages innovation and clean design. This site pays tribute to that spirit by keeping things bold, minimal, and user‑focused.
